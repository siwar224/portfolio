Khidma Marketplace Full Stack

Khidma

Trusted Home Services, Simply.

A Tunisian home-services marketplace that helps clients describe a need, compare proposals from verified professionals, and follow the intervention from booking to completion.

Explore the case study View workflow

Project Overview

Problem

Finding a reliable home-service professional can be fragmented, unclear, and difficult to coordinate from the first request to the final intervention.

Solution

Khidma centralizes service discovery, client requests, professional proposals, appointment tracking, notifications, and private conversations.

Engineering Focus

A modular React and Fastify application with role-based access, secure sessions, real-time events, validated APIs, and MongoDB-backed workflows.

Marketplace Workflow

01Publish a service request
02Receive professional proposals
03Select the right professional
04Confirm and track the appointment
05Complete the intervention

Technical Highlights

Secure access

Client, professional, and admin roles with JWT access and refresh tokens, e-mail OTP verification, password reset, and refresh-token revocation.

Real-time collaboration

Persisted notifications with Socket.IO, plus private Stream Chat conversations linked to requests and proposals.

Privacy by design

Exact addresses and personal contact details remain hidden before appointment confirmation, while access is controlled by role and business status.

Technical Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, custom CSS, Fastify, Node.js, MongoDB, Mongoose, Zod, JWT, Bcrypt, Nodemailer/SMTP, Socket.IO, Stream Chat, Swagger/OpenAPI, Vitest, and MongoDB Memory Server.

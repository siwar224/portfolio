Watch Demo View Screens

Project Overview

Problem

Learning platforms need to support different roles, live sessions, course organization, questions, notifications, and progress tracking without making the experience heavy for users.

Solution

Zad Ezzaytouni centralizes course discovery, student access, teacher dashboards, admin analytics, virtual classrooms, and AI-assisted Q&A inside one cohesive platform.

My Role

I designed and developed full-stack features across React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, secure authentication, dashboards, APIs, AI services, Docker, and CI/CD workflows.

AI Video Dubbing MERN

YouDube

AI-Powered Video Dubbing Platform

A full-stack platform that helps users upload a video, choose a target language and voice, then generate a localized version through an AI processing workflow.

Watch Demo View Screens

Project Overview

Problem

Video creators need a faster way to localize content for different audiences without manually handling transcription, translation, audio generation, and final video assembly.

Solution

YouDube provides a guided workflow for uploading video content, selecting a dialect and voice, launching the AI pipeline, and managing the resulting projects from a dashboard.

My Role

I worked across the full stack: responsive React interfaces, REST APIs, authentication, file workflows, and integration with a dedicated AI processing service.
